FAQs

Answers to common questions

What types of websites do you develop?
We develop business websites, educational websites, portfolio websites, landing pages, e-commerce websites and custom web applications — all tailored to your requirements.
How long does website development take?
Timelines depend on project scope and complexity. A starter website typically takes less time, while custom solutions take longer. We share a clear timeline during the planning phase.
Do you provide domain registration?
We help configure and connect your domain. Domain registration specifics can be discussed during the project planning phase.
Do you provide database solutions?
Yes. We build secure, structured database systems for managing student, customer and business information, including cloud databases and backup solutions.
Can I request new features later?
Yes. We support new feature additions even after launch. Features can be added as part of a maintenance plan or as a separate request.
Do you provide website maintenance?
Yes. We offer website maintenance including updates, bug fixes, feature additions, performance improvements and technical support.
Can you integrate payment gateways?
Yes. We integrate supported online payment solutions with secure checkout and reliable processing.
Can I request a custom package?
Absolutely. If our packages don't fit your needs, request a custom quote and we'll tailor a solution around your requirements.
